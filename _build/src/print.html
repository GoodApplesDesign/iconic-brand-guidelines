These are the foundations of the ICONIC identity: our logo, colour and typography, and how they come together.

Use them whenever you make something for ICONIC, whether you sit on the team, at an agency or with one of our partners. If something isn’t covered here, talk to the ICONIC team before you start.

Logo

The founder’s ring has been part of ICONIC since day one, and the buyers, venue managers and partners we work with already know it. This evolution keeps that equity and gives it a more confident, premium finish.

A refined ring, a new wordmark and a flexible suite of lockups, so the logo works as hard on a favicon as it does on a bottle neck.

Four lockups cover every placement. Each one is master artwork: never redraw, retype or rearrange its parts.

Lead with the primary stack, and with gold on Noir as the hero version. Move to the wide stack or wordmark when space is horizontal, and to the icon when space is very small.

Every lockup, in every colourway, can be downloaded from the online guidelines.

Primary stack

Truest to the legacy logo, using the full stack of logo elements. Use it as the primary logo wherever possible. Best for larger vertical or square placements.

Wide stack

The horizontal version, using the full stack of logo elements. Best for wide placements such as website headers, email signatures and signage.

Wordmark

Use the standalone wordmark when the ring isn’t needed in context. Also suits horizontal placements, and is more compact than the wide stack.

Icon

The most stripped-back version of the logo. Best for very small placements such as favicons and social avatars. Also works as a stamp, pattern or packaging emboss.

If you’re unsure, use the primary stack.

Only step down to a simpler lockup when the space genuinely calls for it.

Three elements make up the logo: the ring, the ICONIC wordmark and BEVERAGES.

The ring is the founder’s mark, refined with a heavier weight so it holds up at small sizes. It stays open where the wordmark breaks through it. ICONIC is set in Sugar Magic, our display typeface. The spacing between the elements is fixed in the master artwork.

The ring carries the colour.

For hero moments, the gold ring catches two warm lights.

Locked opposite each other, they sweep round the ring together and the gold follows, with a soft glow behind and a fine glisten along the edge. The logo itself moves only with a slow, slight push-in.

Keep it for large, high-impact placements on Noir: screens, video, events and launches. Never use it at small sizes or on photography, and never apply other effects to the logo.

Colour

Monochrome is the foundation. Noir and Blanc carry the identity, with Graphite as a supporting neutral.

Two richer alternates, Brown and Olive, are feature backgrounds, each paired with its own metal. Silver, Bronze and Gold run through the ring as gradients, with flat Bronze Lite and Gold Lite for print and small sizes.

Most of what we make should be Noir and Blanc. The contrast is confident and timeless, and it lets the metals do the talking.

Graphite is the supporting neutral, for secondary surfaces, cards and dividers.

Typography

Two typefaces carry the brand. Sugar Magic is our display serif: refined contrast with timeless elegance, and the voice of the wordmark.

Aileron does the everyday work. It is clean, modern and easy to read at any size, so the display type always has room to stand out.

Aa

Sugar Magic Serif Medium sits in the sweet spot between a sophisticated editorial serif and a more modern finish. It is the typeface of the ICONIC wordmark.

It’s a commercial font, so each team using it needs its own licence.

Use it for page titles, headings and short statements, always in capitals: +60 tracking at display sizes and +100 for headings. Never set it in lowercase, and never use it for body copy.

A few habits keep our type looking considered.

Alignment

Set copy left aligned and ragged right. Centre only short, single-line statements. Never justify.

Case

Capitals belong to Sugar Magic alone. Aileron is always set in sentence case, labels and buttons included.

Measure

Keep lines between 45 and 75 characters. Shorter for intros, never edge to edge.

Leading

About 1.1 for display, 1.2 for headings and 1.5 to 1.6 for body copy.

Tracking

Track Sugar Magic at +100, or +60 at display sizes. Leave Aileron at 0.

Colour

Blanc or Noir for copy. Gold Lite for the odd label or accent, never for paragraphs.
