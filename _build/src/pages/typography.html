Sugar Magic Serif Medium sits in the sweet spot between a sophisticated editorial serif and a more modern finish. It is the typeface of the ICONIC wordmark. It’s a commercial font, so each team using it needs its own licence.

Use it for page titles, headings and short statements, always in capitals: +60 tracking at display sizes and +100 for headings. Never set it in lowercase, and never use it for body copy.

Typesetting

A few habits keep our type looking considered.

Alignment

Set copy left aligned and ragged right. Centre only short, single-line statements. Never justify.

Case

Capitals belong to Sugar Magic alone. Aileron is always set in sentence case, labels and buttons included.

Measure

Keep lines between 45 and 75 characters. Shorter for intros, never edge to edge.

Leading

About 1.1 for display, 1.2 for headings and 1.5 to 1.6 for body copy.

Tracking

Track Sugar Magic at +100, or +60 at display sizes. Leave Aileron at 0.

Colour

Blanc or Noir for copy. Gold Lite for the odd label or accent, never for paragraphs.
