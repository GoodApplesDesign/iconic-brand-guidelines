Brand guidelines

The founder’s ring has been part of ICONIC since day one, and the buyers, venue managers and partners we work with already know it. This evolution keeps that equity and gives it a more confident, premium finish.

A refined ring, a new wordmark and a flexible suite of lockups, so the logo works as hard on a favicon as it does on a bottle neck.

Four lockups cover every placement. Each one is master artwork: never redraw, retype or rearrange its parts. Download any lockup, in every colourway, from its card.

Lead with the primary stack, and with gold on Noir as the hero version. Move to the wide stack or wordmark when space is horizontal, and to the icon when space is very small.

If you’re unsure, use the primary stack. Only step down to a simpler lockup when the space genuinely calls for it.

Three elements make up the logo: the ring, the ICONIC wordmark and BEVERAGES.

The ring is the founder’s mark, refined with a heavier weight so it holds up at small sizes. It stays open where the wordmark breaks through it. ICONIC is set in Sugar Magic, our display typeface. The spacing between the elements is fixed in the master artwork.

The ring carries the colour. Each brand background has its own metal, and the wordmark stays Blanc on dark backgrounds and Noir on light ones.

On photography

Use the Blanc logo on dark or busy images and the Noir logo on light, calm ones. The gold logo can sit on deep, calm shadow. Always place the logo over the quietest part of the frame.

Blanc, on a dark image
Noir, on a light image

Keep the area around the logo clear of text, graphics and edges, so the logo always reads on its own.

The logo measures itself. X is the height of the O in ICONIC. Leave at least two O’s (2X) of clear space on every side of the primary stack, wide stack and wordmark, and one (X) around the icon.

Primary stack: 2X
Wide stack: 2X
Wordmark: 2X
Icon: X

Don’t reproduce the logo smaller than these widths, or BEVERAGES stops being legible. When space is tighter, switch to a simpler lockup rather than shrinking.

For hero moments, the gold ring catches two warm lights. Locked opposite each other, they sweep round the ring together and the gold follows, with a soft glow behind and a fine glisten along the edge. The logo itself moves only with a slow, slight push-in.

Keep it for large, high-impact placements on Noir: screens, video, events and launches. Never use it at small sizes or on photography, and never apply other effects to the logo.

When ICONIC appears alongside the brands we represent, or with a venue or event partner, both logos should feel like equals.

Match the ICONIC lockup to the shape of the partner logo, use it in Blanc or Noir at the same optical size, and separate the two with a thin vertical rule. Keep X of clear space on either side of the rule.
